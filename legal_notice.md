# Legal Notice

## Purpose

This system is a passive IoT deception sensor used for cyber-threat research and early-warning intelligence. It does not exploit, attack, or interfere with any system.

## Data Handling

- **IP Addresses**: Stored hashed or partially masked.
- **Personal Data**: No personal data is targeted or intentionally stored.
- **Retention**: Automatic deletion of raw logs after 30 days.
- **Malware Samples**: Stored for research purposes only, never executed.
